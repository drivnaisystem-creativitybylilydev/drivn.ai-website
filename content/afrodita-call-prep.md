- **FAQ content on your website** — AI models pull direct answers from well-structured FAQ pages
- **Mentions in authoritative articles** — being quoted in coaching industry publications, .org domains, Hochschulseiten
- **Reddit threads** — r/executivecoaching, r/coaching — helpful answers from a real practitioner get cited

**What doesn't work:**
- Keyword-stuffing and classic SEO alone
- Presence without depth — a LinkedIn profile with no content
- A website that talks about you but doesn't answer the questions a potential client would actually type into ChatGPT

**The GEO insight to land:**

> "Der Unterschied zu klassischem SEO: Bei Google optimierst Du für Keywords. Das nennt sich GEO, Generative Engine Optimization. Die Grundlage ist, dass Dein Profil und Deine Inhalte konsistent sagen: 'Ich bin die Person, die Führungskräften in dieser Situation hilft.' Überall — Website, ICF-Verzeichnis, LinkedIn, potentiell Reddit."
